Tinbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Tinbox is a Game Boy / Game Boy Advance emulator for iPhone by Redfern's Outpost.

What Tinbox collects

Nothing. Tinbox has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no telemetry. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

  • The game files (ROMs) you import, your in-game saves, save states, cheats, screenshots and
settings are stored in the app's own folder on your iPhone (visible in the Files app under *On My iPhone › Tinbox*) or, if you choose one, in a folder you select. Deleting the app deletes the files in its own folder; Tinbox offers a Backup feature so you can keep a copy elsewhere.
  • Motion sensor data (for games that use tilt or gyro cartridges) is used live and never stored.

Optional network features

Tinbox only uses the network when you turn on or use one of these features:

  • Box art (Settings › Library › *Box art*, on by default). For games that have no cover,
Tinbox requests an image from the open-source *libretro-thumbnails* project hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). The request contains only the game's file name. You can turn this off at any time; it is never used for anything else.
  • RetroAchievements (optional sign-in). If you sign in, your username and password are sent
directly and only to retroachievements.org to obtain a session token, which is stored on your device so you stay signed in. Tinbox then requests achievement lists for the games you play. Signing out deletes the token. RetroAchievements' own privacy policy applies to their service.

No other servers are contacted. Tinbox never uploads your games or saves.

Camera

If you play a Game Boy Camera cartridge, Tinbox asks for camera permission the first time the

cartridge requests a frame. Camera frames are handed directly to the emulated cartridge on your device, exactly like pointing the original hardware at something. Nothing is stored by Tinbox (the game itself saves its photos into its own battery save) and nothing ever leaves your phone. No other feature uses the camera.

Children

Tinbox does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the date above

updated.
